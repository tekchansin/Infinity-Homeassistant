# Changelog

## 14.0-beta8
Your DAP, from anywhere.

**Highlights**
- Link Anywhere reaches your DAP too: switch it on in Settings › Mobile App and on the DAP (Anantar Ultimate 14.0-beta3, Infinity OS or Infinity Pure) and Infinity Core plays to it, opens its Wi-Fi Transfer page and sends music with Sync to DAP from anywhere — through your own Tailscale network.

**Improvements**
- When the output can't play — none chosen, a DAC unplugged, a speaker or a DAP no longer there — the device list opens and says why. Pick another and the song you chose plays there, from where it was.
