# Changelog

## 14.0-beta12
Last.fm, your queue carried over to the DAP, and music copied from the DAP to your computer.

**Highlights**
- Last.fm: connect your account in Settings › Services › Last.fm and what Infinity Core plays goes to your Last.fm profile — "now playing" while it plays, a scrobble once you have heard it. You allow Infinity Core on Last.fm's own page; it never sees your password. A ♡ on a track can love it on Last.fm too. A DAP on Infinity Link scrobbles what it plays itself, so no play counts twice.
- Your queue goes with you to the DAP: choose an Infinity OS or Infinity Pure DAP as the output and the music carries on there. When the DAP has a queue of its own, Infinity Core asks which one to use — Keep my queue, or Import the DAP's queue. Move the music elsewhere, quit Infinity Core or let the computer sleep, and the DAP can keep your queue, so you pick it up on the DAP with Infinity Core closed. With Anantar Ultimate 14.0-beta10 or later.
- Copy music from a DAP to this computer: Sync › Copy from your DAP brings the DAP's music into a music folder here, and ⋯ › Copy to this computer does it for one album or artist that is only on the DAP. Nothing on the computer is ever replaced or removed.
- One row per song: a song you have in several places — this computer, a DAP, a media server, Qobuz, TIDAL — is one row in Tracks, with a mark for each place. ⋯ › Play from picks another copy, and Edit tags opens every copy you can change, so they stay alike.

**New**
- Sync page, redesigned: this computer and the DAP at the top, one card for each way — Send and Copy. A whole-library send or copy first shows how many files, how big and about how long, then waits for Start.
- Lossless Compression for Link Anywhere: to a phone or DAP away from home the music travels as FLAC — bit for bit the same sound, in about half the data. The signal path says when it is in use. Switch it on in the Infinity Link app (14.0-beta8 or later) or on the DAP (Anantar Ultimate 14.0-beta10 or later).
- Wi-Fi Transfer: Download a file from the DAP, or a folder as a .zip.
- Last.fm: a track by several artists goes under the first one, as Last.fm expects.

**Improvements**
- A DAP on Infinity Link chooses by itself how a track plays: its own file, or its own Qobuz / TIDAL, when the sound is the same — streamed from Infinity Core when you use upsampling, DSP or crossfade here. The "Plays what it has itself" switch is gone.
- Remote access, the phone app and browsers on your network open a big library much sooner, with a fraction of the data.
- Sending or copying music gives way to playback: while music plays to an Infinity Link device, a transfer slows down.
- A DAP shown as Not connected in the device list connects when you choose it again.

**Fixes**
- After you quit Infinity Core, the DAP went back to the queue it had before, as if the music you had played never was. It keeps your queue now.
- A DAP at home stays on your home network: a short restart of the DAP could move its music onto Link Anywhere, and it stayed there.
