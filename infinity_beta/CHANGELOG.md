# Changelog

## 14.0-beta6
Your music on your phone — at home and away.

**Highlights**
- Infinity Link for Android is now the remote too: browse, choose what plays and where, and play the music on the phone itself. Install it from the QR code in Settings › Infinity Link, then pair it once with a code from there.
- Link Anywhere: switch it on in Settings › Infinity Link and sign in with your own Tailscale account. Infinity Link on your phone then reaches this Infinity Core from anywhere — on mobile data or any Wi-Fi — and plays its music there. Nothing to set up on your router, encrypted end to end.
- Backups every day: Infinity Core keeps the last 7. Google Drive keeps the last 7 daily backups plus the last 4 you made.
- Change log: the notes of every version in Settings › About › Change log, and on the phone in Settings › Change log.

**Improvements**
- Settings are easier to read: each group in its own titled panel. Remote access is now called Web remote access. Settings › Infinity Link holds the phone app, the phones paired with this Infinity Core and Link Anywhere, with what keeps them private and secure.
- Settings › Infinity Core › Share this Infinity Core: lets your other Infinity Cores use this one.
- On the phone page: swipe back as on Infinity OS; Home, Library, Playlists and More keep their place between tabs; Sound and Precision Upsampling open over Now Playing; Qobuz and TIDAL show their own marks under More.

**Fixes**
- A phone playing over Infinity Link: the position keeps running while the phone switches between Wi-Fi and mobile data, instead of stopping and then jumping.
