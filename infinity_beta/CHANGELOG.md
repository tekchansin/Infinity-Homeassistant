# Changelog

## 14.0-beta4
Likes and plays shared with your DAP, an All artists view, and clearer wording in every language.

**Highlights**
- Likes and play counts sync with your Infinity OS or Infinity Pure DAP over Infinity Link: like a song on either side and it is liked on both, and plays on each side add up. Needs Anantar Ultimate V14.0-beta2 on the DAP.
- Artists: a new "Album artists | All artists" switch. All artists also lists track and featured artists.
- Tags with several artists separated by ";" or "\" (Mp3tag's separator, shown as "₩₩" on Korean Windows) now list each artist on its own.

**Also new**
- Settings › Rebuild library reads every file's tags again. Your likes, plays and playlists stay.
- Thai, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean: every screen reviewed, with clearer and more natural wording.

**Fixes**
- First start: "Add a music folder" opens Settings › Storage, where the music folders now live.
- Mac: while another app plays through the Infinity Core sound card, the play/pause keys, Control Center and AirPods' buttons stay with that app.
- The Discord link goes to our new server.
