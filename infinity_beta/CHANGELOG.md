# Changelog

## 14.0-beta11
One artist per name, one card per release, and even loudness for every file.

**Highlights**
- Volume leveling for files without ReplayGain: Infinity Core measures them itself, quietly in the background, so every track and album plays at the same loudness. On by default — Settings › Audio › Measure tracks without ReplayGain.
- One artist, however it is spelled: "Dead by April" and "Dead By April", "Deform" and "DEFORM" are one artist now, with all their albums on one page. Your files' tags are not changed.
- One card per release on Qobuz and TIDAL artist pages: a release the service lists several times — Hi-Res and CD, Explicit and Clean, Dolby Atmos — shows once, in its best quality. Discography still lists every version.

**New**
- Settings has a search box: type a word to find the setting.
- A first scan shows your music as it reads it, instead of an empty library until the end, and a rescan says how far it is: "12,000 of 350,000".
- A DAP on Infinity Link: the volume shows and moves in the DAP's own steps — 0–63 on a DX270, the same number as on the DAP. With Anantar Ultimate 14.0-beta7 or later.

**Improvements**
- TAK, CAF, AC-3, DTS, Wave64 and AU files show their title, artist and album from their tags (they showed the file name), and a Matroska file shows the title saved in it.
