# Changelog

## 14.0-beta23
See everything Infinity Core is connected to, in one picture.

**Highlights**
- Settings › Overview: one picture of your Core — what you control it from, where your music comes from, where it plays and what it reaches online. A gold line follows the music; click any box for its details and quick switches.
- Phone app: the first Play after you open the app asks where the sound goes — this device or where it plays now.

**Improvements**
- A phone that opens the app appears in the device list at once, without pressing ↻.
- Tag editor: a toolbar instead of Tools — show all, edited or ticked files, a filter, Finder-style clicks; the first columns stay in view.
- Docker, Umbrel and Home Assistant: fixed ports for network players (8950) and AirPlay (8960–8979), easy to allow in a firewall.

**Fixes**
- Network players (UPnP): albums no longer skip or stop mid-song; a long-paused Qobuz track plays again.
- A seek on a DAP that plays its own music no longer flashes 0:00.
- AI through Remote access answers again instead of giving up after a minute.
