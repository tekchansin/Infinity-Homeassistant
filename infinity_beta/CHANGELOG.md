# Changelog

## 14.0-beta13
Share this computer's DAC with every Infinity Core in the house.

**Highlights**
- Share this computer's devices: Settings › Audio › Share this computer's devices. Switch on a DAC — or the speakers, or headphones — and every other Infinity Core in the house (Home Assistant, another Mac, a PC) lists it under Infinity Link and plays to it, like an iBasso DAP: bit-perfect, and DSD as DoP for a DAC that takes it. It keeps playing with Infinity Core closed, as long as the Mac is awake.
- The last Play wins: whoever presses Play last has the device, this Mac's own Infinity Core too. The device list says who plays there now — "Playing from Home Assistant" — with Take back.

**Improvements**
- Link Anywhere on mobile data plays steadier on a phone with its screen off: the phone keeps more music ahead, a slow next track gets a head start, and a connection that has stopped carrying music is made again.
- The signal path says "mobile data" for a phone on a cellular network, and the Lossless Compression badge says what it saves: "22% less data".
- Backups: one status card — your last backup and Back up now — and one list for this Mac and Google Drive.
- Pages fill the window on a big screen.
- Qobuz and TIDAL show in the sidebar only while you are signed in.
- The phone page's device list has the same icons as here, marks the phone in your hand as "This device", and names the computer the music is on — Home Assistant's devices under "Home Assistant".
- When a Wi-Fi Transfer download is finished, Infinity Core says so.
- Settings › About: the Licence agreement.

**Fixes**
- A Wi-Fi Transfer download cut short (the DAP stopped, Wi-Fi dropped) was saved as if whole — a .zip that would not open. It is marked as failed now and the broken file is removed.
- A device connected on your home network could be moved onto Link Anywhere while it played.
- My Albums jumped by itself after a fast scroll.
- The phone page: a page that would not scroll after Back.
- Home Assistant and Linux: of two outputs with the same name, the second one played on the first.
