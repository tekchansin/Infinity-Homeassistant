# Changelog

## 14.0-beta32
Autoplay and queue changes reach UPnP streamers.

**Fixes**
- UPnP streamers: Autoplay goes on after the last track, and Play next, remove or move in the queue reach the streamer — some stopped at the end of the last track instead.
- UPnP streamers that forget the track when it ends go on to the next song instead of pausing.
