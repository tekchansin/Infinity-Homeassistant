# Changelog

## 14.0-beta14
A shorter device list.

**Highlights**
- Hide the devices you never play to: open the device list, tap **Edit list** at the bottom, then tap a row to hide it — or to bring it back. A box with many outputs (HDMI ports, Loopback cables, a building full of AirPlay speakers) shows only the ones you use. The list is the same on every phone and browser that uses this Infinity Core; the device playing now can't be hidden.
